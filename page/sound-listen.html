Where should the castle's sounds come from?

Ten short clips from free, public-domain libraries and two made in code. Tap play on each. Quiet at first, a clip is only a few seconds. What recording at home or a sound generator would give cannot be sampled free, so it is not here.

door

an old door closing. Stands for: a heavy door on its hinge. Licence: Public domain.

creak

a handle creaking. Stands for: old wood creaking. Licence: Public domain.

latch

a rusty clasp. Stands for: a latch lifting, a chain. Licence: Public domain.

fire

wood fire crackling. Stands for: a fire settling in the grate. Licence: Public domain.

drip

one drop. Stands for: water dripping into a basin. Licence: CC0.

trickle

a small trickle. Stands for: water trickling down a stone wall. Licence: CC0.

bell

a church bell ringing. Stands for: the bell, far off. Licence: CC0.

rain

rain, a rumble of thunder. Stands for: rain against the glass. Licence: Public domain.

burn

a stream close by. Stands for: a Highland burn over stones (grounds). Licence: CC0.

birds

birds in a garden. Stands for: birds in the hedges (grounds). Licence: Public domain.

code drip

made in code. Stands for: water dripping into a basin. Licence: ours.

code wind

made in code. Stands for: a thin whistle in a chimney in a gale. Licence: ours.
